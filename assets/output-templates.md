# Output templates

Use only fields that help the assignment. Replace bracketed text with confirmed content, mark assumptions, and remove unused optional rows. These are editable templates; they are not approved client copy.

## Short creator brief

**Campaign / product:** [name and source]

**Market, audience, language:** [market] / [audience] / [language and register]

**Goal:** [what the campaign should achieve]

**Core message:** [one supportable message]

**Required / avoid:** [requirements] / [restrictions]

**Creator concept:** [one-sentence creative direction; preserve creator voice]

**Proof or demo:** [what can be shown, with evidence source]

**CTA / offer conditions:** [exact CTA and applicable conditions]

**Deliverables and timing:** [format, runtime, platform, due dates if confirmed]

**Review and approval:** [reviewers, sequence, final approver; pending if unknown]

**Open decisions:** [owner and question]

## Script review note

| Priority | Location / original | Finding and reason | Suggested edit or option | Decision owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [High / Medium / Low / Preference] | [shot or exact line] | [brief link, evidence, or feasibility reason] | [specific supported edit; otherwise question] | [brand / creator / agency / pending] |

Priority describes the consequence for this deliverable, not predicted campaign performance. Use “Preference” for subjective voice or style notes without a clear requirement.
