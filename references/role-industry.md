# Role and industry routing

Identify both axes independently. Do not infer authority from job title alone; use the explicit assignment and approval map.

## Collaboration role

- **Brand/growth client-side (甲方):** Emphasize the campaign objective, audience, budget or constraints when provided, brand guardrails, required evidence, and acceptance criteria. Surface decisions the client must make. Do not silently approve the work.
- **Agency execution partner (乙方 agency):** Emphasize deliverable readiness, shoot feasibility, script and asset review, revision tracking, creator coordination within the user's stated remit, and a clear approval trail. Coordinate only within the user's established authorization; do not send or commit externally without authorization.
- **Consultant/advisor (乙方 consultant):** Emphasize positioning, route to market, channel choices, and experiments with a learning question and success signal. Avoid imposing shot-by-shot production requirements unless the user requests execution support.
- **Mixed or unclear role:** State the assumed hat and separate advice from execution. Ask who owns approval if that answer materially changes the output.

These are default emphases, not rigid job descriptions. A specific user request, contract scope, or brief can narrow or expand the assignment.

## Client industry tendencies

- **E-commerce / consumer goods:** Often benefits from creator breadth and long-tail creator testing, visible use or demonstration, and a direct conversion CTA. Align the CTA to the actual offer, channel, and goal. Treat scale and conversion as defaults only when the brief supports them.
- **Hard-tech / consumer electronics:** Make practical value observable through a credible use case or demonstration while retaining the brand's intended tone. Verify technical claims against supplied authoritative product material; do not infer performance from appearance.
- **AI products:** Tie the story to a real, relevant trend only when the brief calls for it. Explain a supported change to a workflow or task with a concrete example. Do not claim a product is revolutionary, replaces people, or guarantees outcomes without substantiation.

Industry tendencies never override the brief, campaign goal, market, creator fit, or user direction. Ask for evidence when a claim would otherwise be material or comparative.
